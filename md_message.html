A Message From Our Leadership

Managing Director & Lead Consultant Message

Dr. Md Anwar Shafique

MSS, MBA, MPhil, PhD

Managing Director & Lead Consultant
Centre for Innovation and Research

Welcome to the Center for Innovation and Research (CIR)! It is with great enthusiasm that I embrace you at the CIR—a hub where creativity meets cutting-edge technology, and ideas transform into impactful solutions.

The ambition of the center is to create knowledge through academic and applied research and disseminate acquired knowledge through training and publication. CIR, an emerging research organization, is a research, training and human development center which works for the greater well-being of society for achieving sustainable development goals and more.

We believe in the power of collaboration, bringing together bright minds from diverse disciplines to spark creativity and foster impactful research. This center is more than just a space; it's a vibrant ecosystem where curiosity is nurtured, experimentation is encouraged, and innovation thrives.

I am confident that together, we will achieve remarkable things. I look forward to witnessing the groundbreaking discoveries and transformative innovations that will emerge from the Center for Innovation and Research.

Welcome on the knowledge ship! Let's shape the future together.

— Warm Regards, Dr. Md Anwar Shafique
